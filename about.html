Our Mission

At Spectril, we are dedicated to advancing environmental science through cutting-edge technology. Our mission is to develop high-precision Raman spectrophotometers that provide detailed chemical and molecular analysis for a wide range of applications. We believe that by making this technology more accessible and powerful, we can contribute to a cleaner, healthier planet.

The Founder

Michael Nehring, the Founder & Principal Engineer of Spectril, is a passionate innovator with a deep commitment to environmental stewardship. With a background in engineering and a drive to solve complex challenges, Michael leads our team in pushing the boundaries of what's possible in spectroscopic analysis.
